### Survey  
To gather primary data on the organizational development needs and attitudes of Hungarian SMEs, we designed and deployed a targeted survey. The questionnaire assesses both the current status and the willingness of these companies to engage in various organizational development initiatives.

The survey includes the following key questions:

1. **To what extent do you have visibility into how efficiently your organization/business operates (in terms of processes, collaboration, and organizational culture)?**  
   (Scale: 1 - Not at all, to 5 - Fully understand)

2. **What challenges are you currently facing within your organization?**  
   (Multiple-choice; options include communication problems, lack of process transparency, tensions from growth, key personnel overload, employee skill gaps, leadership generational change, financial difficulties, organizational growth challenges, or no challenges.)

3. **Have you ever used organizational development services (e.g., organizational diagnostics, consultancy, coaching, HR audits, training)?**  
   (Options: Yes, regularly; Yes, occasionally; No, but I have considered it; No, and do not plan to; I am not aware of these services.)

4. **How open would you be to trying an automated, digital organizational diagnostic tool that provides a fast, affordable, and immediate overview of your organization's condition?**  
   (Scale: 1 - Not open at all, to 5 - Very open)

5. **What question or challenge would you like to address or solve through organizational development?**  
   (Open-ended)

6. **What amount would you be willing to pay for a product that offers a solution to your organizational development needs?**  
   (Options range from 5,000 to over 250,000 HUF, or not willing to pay.)

7. **What is the size of your organization?**  
   (Options: 1–9 employees, 10–49, 50–99, 100–250)

8. **In which sector does your organization primarily operate?**  
   (Options include manufacturing/production, services, trade (wholesale or retail), mixed (both manufacturing and services), or other.)

The survey was distributed to SME leaders and decision-makers primarily through social media platforms and professional groups, targeting respondents knowledgeable about their company's current organizational status and development needs.

These survey responses complement the official statistical data, providing valuable insights into the real challenges, openness, and priorities of Hungarian SMEs regarding organizational development.
